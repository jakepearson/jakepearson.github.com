Total time: 40 minutes. Yield: 6 servings.

## Ingredients

- 3 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 1½-inch chunks
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter, cubed
- 1 cup whole milk or half-and-half, warmed
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Put the potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water by 1 inch. Add 1 tablespoon salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook until a knife slides in with no resistance, 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Drain well and return the potatoes to the hot pot over low heat for 1 minute to steam off excess moisture.
4. Mash with a potato masher or pass through a ricer. Avoid a food processor, which turns them gluey.
5. Stir in the butter until melted, then add the warm milk a little at a time until creamy.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot.

## Notes

- Warm the milk before adding it so the potatoes stay hot and absorb it evenly.
- For extra flavor, simmer 3 smashed garlic cloves with the potatoes and mash them in.
