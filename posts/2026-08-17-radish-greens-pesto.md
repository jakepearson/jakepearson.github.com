From [Carol, From A Chef's Kitchen](https://www.fromachefskitchen.com/radish-greens-pesto/). Total time: 30 minutes. Yield: approximately 2 cups (4 servings).

## Ingredients

- 4 cups (packed) radish tops, washed and dried
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- ½ large lemon, juiced (approximately 2 tablespoons)
- ½ cup sliced or slivered almonds, coarsely chopped macadamia nuts, pistachios, or pine nuts
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil, plus more as needed
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine the first 6 ingredients in a food processor or blender (work in batches if necessary).
2. Process until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
3. Add additional olive oil to achieve a thick sauce consistency.
4. Season to taste with salt and black pepper.
